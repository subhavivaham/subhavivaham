# Subha Vivaham — Premium Tamil Matrimony Platform

**Subha Vivaham** ("சுப விவாஹம்") is a premium Tamil Nadu matrimony platform built around a simple promise: *Traditional Values. Meaningful Matches.* It pairs a calm, heritage-inspired interface with a modern, distraction-free experience — helping Tamil families find a life partner with confidence, dignity and warmth.

The product blends an editorial marketing site, a gated members' area, and a full administrative console into one cohesive, responsive web application.

---

## The Experience

**For visitors, the journey is open and inviting.** The home page sets the tone with a full-bleed hero, a "why us" story, a how-it-works flow, featured success stories, membership pricing and a trust-and-FAQ section. Visitors can browse the Tamil temple directory, explore marriage services and vendor listings, read published success stories, and try the Jathagam (natchathram porutham) calculator before ever signing up.

**For members, the experience becomes personal.** After a guided multi-step registration, members land in a focused members' area with a persistent sidebar, a personal dashboard, daily matches, a send/receive interests inbox, a rich profile viewer and a profile editor. Everything is geared toward one outcome — the right introduction.

**For the families behind every profile**, the interface stays respectful and private: contact details are never public, and visibility is controlled and deliberate.

## A Premium UI Design

The design language is deliberate and consistent across every screen, tuned to feel established, trustworthy and quietly luxurious.

- **A heritage palette.** A deep **maroon** brand colour anchors the interface, lifted by **gold** accents and set on soft **ivory** backgrounds with charcoal text — a combination that reads as traditional yet contemporary.
- **Editorial typography.** **Playfair Display** carries headings with an elegant, magazine-like weight, while **Inter** keeps body copy clean and highly readable at every size.
- **Considered surfaces.** Generous spacing, softly rounded cards, hairline borders, layered shadows and subtle hover states give the interface a tactile, premium feel without ever feeling busy.
- **Purposeful motion.** Gentle reveal, fade and slide animations guide the eye and reward interaction, always restrained and never distracting.
- **Bilingual warmth.** Tamil and English are woven together naturally — from the tagline to community names — honouring the audience the platform serves.
- **Fully responsive.** Every page is tuned for phone, tablet and desktop, with dedicated mobile navigation, a collapsible members' sidebar, and a slide-in admin drawer.
- **Trust by design.** Private contact details, clear review states and calm empty states keep the experience reassuring at every step.

The visual identity — logo, colour tokens, imagery treatment and components — is centralised so the premium look stays consistent as the product grows.

## Membership

Membership is presented as a simple, honest upgrade path: a free tier to begin, and paid tiers that unlock more photos, more interests and direct contact access. Pricing is comparison-first — what's included at each level is shown plainly, and upgrading is a short, guided flow rather than a checkout maze.

## Running Locally

```bash
npm run lint     # lint the project
```

Open http://localhost:3000 after starting the dev server.
